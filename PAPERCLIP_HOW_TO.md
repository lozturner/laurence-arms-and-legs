# 📎 Paperclip

## How to install and run it

**Double-click `INSTALL_AND_RUN.bat`.**

That's it.

It installs Python if you don't have it, installs everything Paperclip needs, sets it to launch automatically every time Windows starts, and launches it right now.

Wait ~30 seconds. A grey paperclip appears top-left of your screen. A paperclip icon appears in the system tray (bottom-right by the clock — click the `^` arrow if hidden).

## How to use it

**Right-click three times fast. Anywhere on the screen.**

The red number on the floating paperclip goes up by 1. It just saved: the window you were in, the browser tab, the URL, a screenshot, the time.

Click the floating paperclip to see what you've saved.

Every 3–7 minutes a small note pops up reminding you what you pinned. It vanishes on its own.

## How to quit

Right-click the system tray paperclip → Quit.

## Where stuff is saved

`%APPDATA%\paperclip\` — your sessions and screenshots. Open it from the tray menu.
